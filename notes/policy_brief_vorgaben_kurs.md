# Vorgaben der Kursleitung zum Policy Brief

*Vom Nutzer am 15.11.2026 eingefügt, unverändert übernommen.*

Focus on your audience
who are they
what do they know
why is it important for them
→ just provide what they need to know in the brief

logical organization
each paragraph should address only one problem


Summary
Context: Explain problem and why it does matter to the reader → only mention what later on is also getting solved or explained
Project/Aproach: What did I do, build, analyze etc
Findings: What did I learn, also restrictions and boundaries
Implications: What does it mean to the reader or us as unit
Recommandations: What should reader do next, or what need to be done to clarify problems and challenges
